# Music

Optional looping background score on the Music bus. Play it via
`AudioManager.play_music("res://assets/audio/music/<file>.ogg")` (e.g. from Home
or a menu controller). Nothing plays until you call it, so this folder may stay
empty during early development.

Keep it minimal and unobtrusive — think the calm of Monument Valley or Headspace,
not a loop that demands attention. Loop cleanly. Target ~ -20 LUFS.
